# Pixel Passport artwork brief

Original raster art was generated with ImageGen. Character variants used the original traveler-and-cat image as the editing reference; landscape and destination scenes were new generations. All PNG originals remain unchanged. `prepare-art.py` creates WebP delivery copies without cropping or changing the drawings.

The shared prompt direction was: detailed 16-bit storybook pixel art, warm pastel European scenery, crisp intentional pixels, consistent outlines and shading, no text or logos.

- Alpine panorama: Swiss mountains, lake, terracotta village buildings, cherry blossoms, pine forests, train on a stone bridge, pastel morning sky.
- Destination postcards: Paris and Eiffel Tower; St. Gallen and Swiss architecture; Rome and Colosseum; a four-scene atlas of Vienna, Helsinki, Athens, and Venice; snowy Finland with pine forests, village lights, and Northern Lights.
- Character: long wavy brown hair, pink knitted beanie, cream travel jacket, olive backpack, small white cat. Six expression poses, six outfits, six action poses, and four turnaround views.
- Inventory atlas: 6 × 4 transparent cells of shaded travel and packing objects, including navigation items, tickets, transport, camera, clothing, ski equipment, toiletries, electronics, passport, bottle, and adapter.
- Floral atlas: transparent corner clusters of alpine bushes and flowers, a cherry blossom branch, and a pine tree with mossy rocks.

SVG files in the repository are original code-drawn UI accents and small symbols. The primary navigation and inventory illustrations use the generated raster atlas.
