# Living world assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Originals live in `source-art/world/`; optimized web copies in `dist/assets/world/`. CSS selects sprite cells without destructive cropping. Mountains and seasonal village art are independent transparent layers. The train, railway, clouds, cat and water accents are repo-native pixel SVG/CSS components.

+### Asset 1
Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset for Pixel Passport, cozy pastel 16-bit pixel art, hard pixel edges, beautiful detailed European Alpine travel scenery. Create one transparent-background atlas arranged in EXACTLY two columns and two rows, four identical-size landscape cells, no separators or text. Each cell is a wide horizontal environment layer: lake in front, quaint Swiss European village with church towers on right, pine forest and hills across left and right, small warm windows; sky and upper half fully TRANSPARENT, no mountains so another independent mountain layer can sit behind. Cell top-left winter: snowy roofs/pines, icy lake, blue and lavender shading. Top-right spring: cherry blossoms, fresh sage green forest, blue lake. Bottom-left summer: rich green trees, warm coastal-looking Alpine village, teal lake. Bottom-right autumn: golden leaves, russet rooftops, amber lake reflections. All four same composition and ground baseline, detailed calm indie-game world, cream/sage/rose palette, winter has luminous pale blues. No train, no railway, no bridge, no characters, no UI, no writing, no watermarks. The cells must stay within their exact quadrant. Transparent alpha everywhere above the village tree skyline.
